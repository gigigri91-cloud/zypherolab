With global mobile traffic consistently outweighing desktop, treating mobile design as an afterthought is a critical error. Mobile-first design isn't just about making things fit on a smaller screen; it's a fundamental shift in how we approach user experience.

## Why Mobile-First Matters

1. **Google's Mobile-First Indexing**: Google predominantly uses the mobile version of the content for indexing and ranking. If your mobile site lacks content or performs poorly, your desktop rankings will suffer too.
2. **User Context**: Mobile users are often on the go, seeking quick answers or immediate actions. Navigation must be intuitive, buttons must be easily tappable, and crucial information must be front and center.
3. **Performance Constraints**: Mobile devices vary wildly in processing power and network speed. Designing for mobile first forces developers to prioritize performance, leading to faster, leaner code that benefits all users.
